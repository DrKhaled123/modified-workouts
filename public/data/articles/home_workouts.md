# Home Workouts: Effectiveness Without Equipment

## Can You Build Muscle at Home?
Yes. 2024 research on "Blood Flow Restriction" (BFR) shows low-load training can match heavy lifting.

## Key Principles
1.  **RIR (Reps In Reserve)**: Train to 0-1 RIR (failure) to compensate for low load.
2.  **Unilateral Training**: Single-leg squats, one-arm pushups.
