# Female Training: Science & Cycle Syncing (2025 Guide)

## The Myth of "Pink Dumbbells"
Modern research confirms: Women should train with the same intensity and compound movements as men. However, hormonal fluctuations require strategic adjustments.

## Menstrual Cycle Syncing
Optimizing training based on your 28-day hormonal rhythm.

### Phase 1: Follicular (Days 1-14)
*   **Hormones**: Estrogen rising.
*   **Performance**: High pain tolerance, faster recovery, better insulin sensitivity.
*   **Focus**: **PR Attempts & High Volume**. Push hard here.
*   **Nutrition**: Higher carbs are well-tolerated.

### Phase 2: Ovulation (Day 14)
*   **Hormones**: Peak Estrogen & Testosterone.
*   **Performance**: Peak strength.
*   **Caution**: ACL laxity increases (higher injury risk). Ensure proper warm-ups.

### Phase 3: Luteal (Days 15-28)
*   **Hormones**: Progesterone rising.
*   **Performance**: Body temperature rises, metabolic rate increases (+100-300 kcal).
*   **Focus**: **Deload & Steady State Cardio**. Strength may dip.
*   **Nutrition**: Prioritize fats and protein; cravings are natural.

## Glute & Lower Body Focus
Women generally have a higher ratio of Type I (slow-twitch) fibers and recover faster between sets.
*   **Strategy**: Use higher rep ranges (15-20) and shorter rest periods (30-60s) for lower body growth.
*   **Key Moves**: Hip Thrusts, RDLs, Bulgarian Split Squats.
