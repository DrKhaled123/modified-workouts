# Recovery Science: The Hidden Half of Training (2024 Update)

## Sleep Architecture & Glymphatic Clearance
Recent 2024 studies highlight the role of the glymphatic system in clearing beta-amyloid during deep sleep.
*   **Deep Sleep (NREM 3)**: Physical restoration. HGH is released here.
*   **REM Sleep**: Mental restoration and motor skill consolidation.

## Central Nervous System (CNS) Fatigue
*   **Symptoms**: Reduced grip strength, HRV (Heart Rate Variability) drop.
*   **Fix**: Deload weeks (reduce volume by 50%) every 6-8 weeks.

## Active Recovery
*   **Protocol**: 20-30 mins of Zone 1 cardio increases blood flow to muscles without causing fatigue.
