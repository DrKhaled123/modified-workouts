# Welcome to Workouts Pro

This application is designed to help you achieve your fitness goals through science-based training plans.

## How to use this app
1. **Choose a Goal**: Select from Bulking, Cutting, or other specialized programs.
2. **Follow the Plan**: Each program has a weekly schedule.
3. **Track Rest**: Use the built-in floating timer to keep your rest periods consistent.

## Nutrition
Nutrition is 70% of the equation. Check the Nutrition tab for guidelines.
