# Cardio Mastery: HIIT vs. LISS (2025 Protocols)

## Zone 2 Training (The Gold Standard)
2025 longevity research emphasizes Zone 2 (60-70% Max HR).
*   **Mechanism**: Improves mitochondrial efficiency and lactate clearance.
*   **Protocol**: 45+ mins steady state, nasal breathing only.

## HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)
*   **Protocol**: 4x4 Method (4 mins hard, 3 mins active rest).
