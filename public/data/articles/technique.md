# Technique & Tempo: The Science of Hypertrophy

## Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy (2024)
New evidence shows training the muscle in its lengthened position (e.g., bottom of a squat, full stretch on flyes) produces 15-20% more growth.

## Tempo
*   **Controlled Eccentric**: 2-3 seconds down is optimal. Slower (5s+) has diminishing returns.
