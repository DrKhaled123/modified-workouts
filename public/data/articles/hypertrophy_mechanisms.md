# Advanced Hypertrophy: Mechanisms of Growth (2025)

## The Three Pillars (Updated)
Dr. Brad Schoenfeld's 2024 meta-analysis re-confirms:

### 1. Mechanical Tension (Primary Driver)
The force generated by the muscle fibers. Stretch-mediated hypertrophy is the current focus (training at long muscle lengths).

### 2. Metabolic Stress
Accumulation of metabolites. Effective, but secondary to tension.

### 3. Muscle Damage
Now considered a byproduct rather than a goal. Excessive damage impairs frequency.

## Volume Recommendations (2025)
*   **Weekly Sets**: 12-24 sets per muscle group per week, titrating up based on recovery.
