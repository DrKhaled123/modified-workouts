# Training During Fasting: Science & Application (2024)

## Biological Mechanisms
*   **Autophagy**: 2024 studies show accelerated cellular cleanup during fasted training.
*   **Lipolysis**: Increased fat oxidation, though total daily energy balance remains king.

## Best Practices
*   **Electrolytes**: Essential during fasting to prevent cramping (Sodium 1000mg).
